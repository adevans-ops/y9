How to use this

  1. Use the numbered steps at the top of the page to move between the three tasks.
  2. Click an email on the left to read it.
  3. Flag it if it needs action from you.
  4. File it into the right folder.
  5. If it has a deadline, add it to your calendar and set a reminder for the day before.
  6. Click Check my work whenever you like — you can always go back and fix things.

Wait — that was a practice phishing link

This email and link were not real. They were built to show what a phishing email can look like.

Real phishing emails often create false urgency, use a generic greeting instead of your name, come from an odd-looking email address, and want you to click a link or hand over a password.

Next time, flag emails like this and check with a teacher before clicking anything.
